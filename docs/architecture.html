The eleven phases

Order matters here: each phase consumes what the one before it produced.

01
Foundation14 Sep 2026
Docker Compose stack: Postgres 16 + pgvector, MLflow, Airflow. uv, pytest, smoke tests.
shipped
02
DLD ingestion15 Sep 2026
Every row loaded and kept, each non-sale carrying exactly one exclusion reason. Area alias table maps “JVC” and “Dubai Marina” to official names. Runs from the CLI or an Airflow trigger.
shipped
03
Price model16 Sep 2026
Homes sold 2015 on, priced relative to a leak-free market index with building → project → area → city fallbacks. Beat a comps baseline by 27% to earn registration.
shipped
04
Duplicate & fraud16 Sep 2026
Synthetic listings over real sales, with planted reposts and two kinds of listing that must not be flagged. Candidates come from pgvector, decisions from twelve signals, fraud flags from the price model and the photo graph.
shipped
05
Search ranking16 Sep 2026
Natural-language property search — “2BR in Dubai Marina under 1.5M”. A rule parser, semantic and full-text retrieval fused by rank, one listing per duplicate cluster, and a LightGBM ranker that had to beat a hand-written rule scorer to earn registration.
shipped
06
Price forecastingnext · added 16 Sep 2026
Separate 3-month, 1-year and 3-year models with walk-forward validation, conformal ranges and sourced infrastructure-pipeline features. A horizon ships only if it beats its accuracy gate.
up next

Build log

16 Sep 2026

Phase 5 shipped. Search over the 20,000 listings, trained and scored on 6,000 rule-graded queries in about 30 minutes. The final review showed a 20-line rule scorer already reaches 0.972 NDCG, so the ranker now has to beat that to be registered (it does, by 0.025). It also fixed an engine that stayed mid-transaction, ordinary words read as building names, and missing community names like “Arabian Ranches 2”. Price forecasting was added to the roadmap as Phase 6.

16 Sep 2026

Phase 4 shipped. 20,000 listings embedded on the GPU and checked in about 21 minutes end to end. The final review found a relist flag that could never fire and same-building controls easier than the spec asked for; both were fixed and the corpus rebuilt. Precision now meets the 98% target; recall fell to 21% and the README says so. Hosting switched from Cloud Run to a free Cloudflare Tunnel.

16 Sep 2026

Phase 4 under way. Photo pool, corpus generator and the listings schema are in. Reviews caught four ground-truth flaws before they could flatter the results — bait prices computed off the wrong base, control groups collapsing into one district, stock photos indistinguishable from ordinary ones, and two columns that gave the answer away for free.

16 Sep 2026

Phase 3 shipped. Price model registered as v2. Beats the comps rule by 27% and LightGBM by 0.4 points. Its 80% price ranges are calibrated on the test period, and the README says plainly where they under-cover.

15 Sep 2026

Phase 2 shipped. The full DLD file loads in 29 seconds, atomically. MLflow's store moved off the OneDrive-synced folder after intermittent 15–20 second stalls.

14 Sep 2026

Phase 1 shipped. Docker Compose stack up, with smoke tests that fail loudly rather than skip when a service is misconfigured.

Real DLD transactions throughout. Listing text, photos and duplicates in phase 4 are synthetic and labelled. Updated as each phase lands.
